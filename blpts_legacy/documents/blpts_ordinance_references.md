# Ordinance References -- BLPTS Fee & Inspection Logic

## Ordinance 2011-07
**Year:** 2011
**Subject:** Fee increase for Retail (A) and Food Service (B) license
categories, +15%, effective renewal years 2011 and later. Home Occupation
(C) and Non-Profit (E) explicitly exempted from this increase.
**Full text on file:** Partial -- page 2 missing from county archive.

## Ordinance 2016-03
**Year:** 2016
**Subject:** Establishes the "Home Occupation Plus" designation for
Home Occupation (C) licenses exceeding standard square footage
thresholds, allowing expanded business activity under the same license
type.
**Full text on file:** Yes.
