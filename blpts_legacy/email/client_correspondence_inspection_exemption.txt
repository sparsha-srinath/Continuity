Hi Anjali,

Following up after you looped in our records office. I spoke with the
County Attorney's staff yesterday and they located the full text of
Ordinance 2009-14 in the archived council minutes (not previously
digitized -- it was only in the physical binder in the clerk's vault).

Confirmed: Ordinance 2009-14, Section 3(b), exempts "low-risk business
categories" -- specifically Home Occupation licenses (our type C) and
Non-Profit licenses (our type E) -- from the standard 30-day
post-application inspection scheduling requirement. The rationale
given in the ordinance is that these categories present minimal
premises risk compared to retail or food service.

So this is NOT a system defect. The exemption is intentional and has a
documented legal basis. I've attached a scanned copy of the relevant
ordinance page to this email for your records (not included in this
export, but the physical/scanned copy is on file with our office as
of today's date).

Please have your team update your documentation to cite Ordinance
2009-14, Section 3(b) directly rather than "per standard practice" --
that phrasing in some of our older internal notes was never accurate
and I'd like to see it retired.

Let me know if you need anything else on this.
